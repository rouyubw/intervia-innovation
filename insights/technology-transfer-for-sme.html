Your Technology Is Worth More Somewhere Else

Asian markets are absorbing technology faster than ever. The opportunity window for SMEs is right now.

May 2026 · 3 min read · Based on WIPO World Intellectual Property Report 2026

Here is a question most SME owners never ask: what if the product or process you built for your home market is exactly what another Asian market is hungry for — and has no access to? The data says that window is wide open. And it is closing.

The WIPO World Intellectual Property Report 2026 just dropped the most comprehensive study of technology diffusion ever published.1 Buried in it is a finding that every business owner in Asia should read.

Technology adoption gaps between advanced economies and Asia have not just narrowed — in digital technologies, Asia has exceeded advanced economy usage levels.2

Asia is not waiting anymore. Markets that once trailed the West by decades are now moving faster. This means the window to bring your technology in early is shorter than it has ever been.
